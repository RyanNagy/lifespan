Molecular Bingo

Generate a set of bingo cards showing skeletal structures. The caller reads out a condensed formula — players find the matching structure. Each card gets a unique randomized layout and a scannable specimen tag so you can pull it back up to verify a win.

1 · Molecule pool

One molecule per line: Name | SMILES | Condensed formula. Edit, add, or remove freely — you need at least 24 for a 5×5 card with a free center space.

2 · Card settings

Verify a card

Scan a card's QR tag with any QR reader, then paste what it decodes to here. This redraws that exact card on screen — no need to reprint or hunt for the original.

Each bingo card prints on its own page, followed by the caller deck.

Bingo cards

Caller deck

Read the condensed formula aloud (or hold it up); players scan their cards for the matching structure. Molecule name shown small, for the host only.
